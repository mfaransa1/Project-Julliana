Privacy

Your reflections deserve care.

This page describes how Juliana currently handles information. It is a plain-language product explanation, not a legal compliance claim.

What is collected

Accounts use a username, email address, password hash, and optional profile image. Signed-in conversations and journal entries are stored so they can be shown back to their owner.

How it is used

Account data supports access to the service. Chat history and journals are displayed only to the account that owns them. Journal entries are not used as model-training data.

Learning and analytics

Automatic learning candidates are disabled by default. If enabled in a future controlled configuration, candidates are privacy-filtered and reviewed; crisis messages are excluded. Administration views are aggregate-only and do not display journal or chat text.

Your controls

You can edit or delete journal entries, clear your conversation history, update account details, and delete your account and associated stored data.

Manage your account

Known limits

Internet services and AI systems have limits. Avoid sharing information you would not want stored, and use the urgent-help guidance when you need immediate human support.
