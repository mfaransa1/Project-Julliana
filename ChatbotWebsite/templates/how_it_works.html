How Juliana works

Designed to respond with care, not certainty.

Juliana uses a local, curated intent system. It does not use an external large-language-model service.

01

Conversation

Juliana processes the message you send and looks for recognised patterns.

02

Context

Short-term context can help Juliana keep a relevant topic within the current conversation.

03

Safety

Safety checks run independently before ordinary conversation responses.

04

Knowledge

Responses come from a curated knowledge base, topics, tests, and mindfulness content.

05

Understanding

Intent, language, and non-clinical emotional signals help select a response.

06

Response

Juliana selects a response or asks for clarification when it is uncertain.

Current, planned, and experimental

Currently implemented: curated intents, language normalisation, basic short-term context, local model inference, and crisis-first checks.

Planned: richer conversation state, better curated knowledge, configurable resources, and additional wellbeing tools.

Experimental: reviewed learning candidates. They are disabled by default and are never a substitute for content review or safety testing.
