About Juliana

A thoughtful space for mental wellbeing.

Why Juliana exists

Mental wellbeing is personal. Finding a comfortable place to talk, reflect, and learn is not always easy. Juliana explores how technology can offer a supportive space while keeping human connection and professional care at the centre.

What Juliana is

A digital mental-wellbeing companion designed for reflection, emotional support, mental-health education, wellbeing exercises, guided conversations, and access to relevant resources.

What Juliana is not

Juliana is not a therapist, psychologist, psychiatrist, doctor, diagnostic system, or emergency service.

Read the safety guidance

From project to platform

Juliana began as a university research and development project exploring conversational AI and mental-wellbeing support. It is now continuing as an ongoing product project exploring contextual conversation, culturally aware support, responsible AI, and privacy-conscious technology.

Juliana does not overstate what it can do. Its role is to offer a supportive starting point and practical tools, while encouraging human and professional support when it is needed.
