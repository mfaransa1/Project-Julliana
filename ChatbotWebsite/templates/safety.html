Safety

When human support matters most.

Juliana is not emergency care.

If you may be in immediate danger, or feel at risk of harming yourself or someone else, seek immediate real-world help. Contact local emergency services, a crisis service, a nearby hospital, or someone you trust.

View available support resources

How Juliana approaches safety

Juliana checks for concerning language separately from ordinary conversation. It may encourage you to seek human support, but its responses can be incomplete or imperfect.

What to do

Use Juliana for reflection and general wellbeing support. For urgent, serious, or worsening concerns, contact a qualified professional or trusted person directly.

Be careful with advice

Juliana does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, contact emergency services, or monitor your safety.
